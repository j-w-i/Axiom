or focus change, then stays solid; with motion Reduced or Off it is solid from the start.

on an idle machine (§Not verified).

## Behaviour implemented
- **The cause, measured:** re_editor's 500 ms caret timer made 21 frames in 10 s.
  - The caret already has its own repaint boundary.
  - Even so, each frame re-rasterises the whole window, about 130 ms of CPU on llvmpipe.
  - Only fewer frames help, and re_editor had no setting for that.
- **The patch** (`third_party/re_editor/PATCHES.md`):
  - `CodeEditor` gets `cursorBlink` and `cursorBlinkTimeout`;
  - the private blink controller honours them;
  - `stopBlink` always hides the caret (upstream returned early when no timer ran);
  - `didUpdateWidget` applies changed settings.
  - The defaults are upstream's behaviour.
- **`AxiomMotion.caretBlinks`:** Standard only.
- **`AxiomMotion.caretBlinkTimeout`:** 10 s, GTK's `gtk-cursor-blink-timeout`.
- **`CodeView`:** passes both to the editor.

## Deviations and decisions
- **The design spec changed (the owner should see this).** At Standard, the editor's caret no
  longer blinks indefinitely. It stays solid after 10 s without input, and any edit, caret move or
  focus change restarts the blink. It was approved by the session that started this task and is
  recorded in workspace-spec.md §2.3 and motion-system.md §5 item 8.
- **The approach.** The task suggested a RepaintBoundary, or a caret that does not drive a
  full-window frame. Neither is possible: the boundary already exists in re_editor, and in Flutter
  any change on screen is a full-window frame on this embedder.
- **A vendored dependency.** It is the same package, version and licence as before, so governance
  rule 1 does not apply (the M5 session's ruling). No upstream issue or PR was opened; that is the
  owner's call.
- **Left out of the vendored copy:** upstream's `example/`, `test/` and `arts/` (PATCHES.md).

## Known defects / limitations
- **The caret is solid during the first 10 s at Reduced and Off.** That is intended: no loop.
- **After focus,** re_editor runs about 0.3 s of its own short fades (seen in the tests). They are
  finite and out of scope.
