## Deviations and decisions
- **A Ninja release needs a manifest change to be offered.** Its digests have to be pinned,
  which is the price of having no checksum file.
- **CMake's "latest" comes from the GitHub releases API** (unauthenticated: 60 requests per hour
  per IP, cached for an hour by the daemon).
