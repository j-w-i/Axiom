## Deviations and decisions
- **Layout store:** the layout lives in a small external store (`LayoutStore`, `src/shell/layout.ts`, with the pure load/migrate/clamp functions): App subscribes only to the two visibility flags, the workbench to the sizes. With the state in App, each drag commit re-rendered the title row, whose Radix dropdowns then committed a second time.
- **Two view fixes outside the scope** (required for acceptance 1): the ResizeObservers in `components/Explorer.tsx` and `components/OutputPanel.tsx` no longer call `setState` when height and scroll position are unchanged; a width-only resize used to commit once per frame (and once more after the drag).
- `--dur-delay: 120ms` added to the motion tokens (the splitter's hover intent; the guard requires durations as tokens).
- "Reset Layout" added to the palette (View group, after Toggle Panel) and the View menu; `tests/shell.test.ts`'s expected command list updated for it (marked in the file).
- The side bar's header title is "Files" with the workspace name as meta (workspace-spec §1); its section is named "Files" (was the `aside` "Explorer").
- Splitter moved from `ui/primitives.tsx` to `ui/panel.tsx`.
- Diagnosis aid used once (not committed): a script wrapping the React DevTools hook during a real XTEST drag showed 0 commits during the drag and 2 after release, which pointed at the title row re-render.
