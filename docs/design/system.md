# Design system

Source of truth: `app/lib/ui/tokens.dart`. Gallery: `app/lib/gallery/`. Images: `flutter test` writes `app/build/shots/` (offscreen).

**The idea.** A professional desktop application in the material of the owner's Unitable (its design system, Temporal Tactility, at the owner's word, 2026-11-25): near-neutral charcoal, depth from tones and one lit edge, what people know from other tools where they expect it, aqua only where something runs or has the keyboard, a service's hue where something is its own.

## Rules
1. **Containment is spent, not given.** A surface may hold another only if the person acts on it as a unit (a key, a request, the composer, a menu, a group of rows). Surfaces do not nest deeper than a card on a ground and a control or a trough on the card.
2. **Raised can be pressed or written into, or is what is chosen; recessed holds something; the rest is ground.** The chassis (the window, the sidebar) and on it the canvas, one tone up, with everything else. Raised: a tone above and soft shades; a trace of light on the upper edge where a tone alone would not do. Recessed (troughs, code, a command that is asked about): a tone below and nothing else. Nothing is drawn round: no ring, no hard line above or below (the owner, of the composer's ring: "not a fan of the border around this"; of it without any edge: "flatter in comparison to some of the other surfaces"). What has the keyboard, and what is wrong, has a ring; a key that cannot be pressed, a faint outline. Tests check it. No gradient, bevel, glass or glow. A shade is drawn with `shaders/shade.frag`, which breaks up its steps: on nearly black there are too few levels for a soft blur, and it shows as rings. Shades are short: none reaches what stands beside it.
2c. **Parts of the window are told apart by tone and room, not by ruled lines.** A hairline is for rows of one kind, inset. What scrolls goes out of sight gradually.
2a. **Corners are continuous and concentric**: an inner corner is as much less round as it stands in.
2b. **From Unitable and not here**, because they are of a timetable or a phone: the track at true scale, the now line, hatched time, stakes, rulers, category colours, the one large answer, the bottom bar and its mark, cards from the bottom, wheels, haptics, targets of 44. Nor its near-black and springs that overshoot: the owner rejected those in v1 (FL-DS-002).
3. **Four inks that recede from each other** (tested): words, quieter words, notes, and, on the grounds only, marks and ages.
4. **Aqua is what runs and what has the keyboard**, and nothing else: not code, not icons, not keys. Keys have no hue; the main act is the lightest thing there is (dark theme) or the darkest (light). The owner's choice of three. Amber asks, red failed, green done; each state has its own shape (tested).
4a. **Things take the hue of the service they belong to.** Its mark, its level, its check, the hover of its menu item. What is selected has one neutral fill, whoever it belongs to. Icons of work have no hue; a failed one is red. Code, diffs and states are in colour. How full a context is, is neutral. Marks identify a service really in use (`THIRD_PARTY.md`); unknown models get none.
4b. **No accent edges.** No coloured strip along any side of a tab, card, row, menu, key or message. Lamps: running is a ring with an arc that goes round, asking a full dot, waiting a small ring, paused two bars, done a check, failed a cross. A test reads the source for one.
4c. **Amber is the lamp, the warning glyph and the one line that says who asks; never a ground.**
5. **One scale of distances** (`AxSpace`): words 24 and blocks and keys 16 from the edge of the canvas; words and blocks 14 from the edge of a card; what is in a row or an item of a menu 10; in a chip 8; what floats holds its items 6 in. The composer, which is rounder, 16. Words never stand nearer than 8 to the side of a surface: every rendered picture is checked for it. **Grid of 4.** What is said, asked and written is one column, 740 at most, in the middle of its room, 24 from the edge at least. What was asked is at the right, as wide as its words, 600 at most. What came of a call stands beside it. Icons are drawn at the size they were made for, 16 (12 for a check and for what folds), in a fixed column: Fluent's outlines, and the full shape for what is open or chosen.
6. **Two voices.** Inter for words; JetBrains Mono, without ligatures, only for literals: code, paths, commands. Both are shipped, so that what is rendered in a test is what a person sees on every system.
7. **Numbers say where they come from.** Solid meter = reported or measured; dashed = estimated; empty track and words = unknown.
8. **Nothing new can be pressed by a click meant for something else** (`Settle`): execution panel, requests. A question falls the moment anything it was about changes.
8a. **A key looks like one.** It has a ground or an outline. Words alone are keys only among others, where they are the lesser of two.
8b. **A row shows its further keys when hovered; state is always shown.**
9. **Things sit inside what owns them.** Sessions under their folder, in the order they were made: opening one or working in it moves nothing. Agents under their session. An agent's views beside its name. What an agent asks is asked in its own conversation, and elsewhere only pointed to. A model and what it can be told behind one key, access beside it, where the agent is spoken to, next to how full its context is. **Models are in one place**: whose they are at the left, which in the middle, found by a word over all of them; what the chosen one can be told at the right and below, and only that. What is selected has a fill, and no strip. The harness follows from the model; it is a choice of its own only for those who turn that on in Settings (0017). Choosing a model or a harness says what that costs and waits for a yes. What belongs to the session and not to the agent that is shown stands in one panel at the right, on the same canvas: what it plans, what has changed, its terminals. No tabs: all of it is in sight, and what is in one opens in place. A file's change opens under its name and the panel widens for it; a terminal opens in a drawer under the conversation. The conversation and what asks stay beside it. Its key stands over it at the far right, as the sidebar's does at the left, and makes all of it disappear, an open terminal too (D); each part folds under its heading. The panel is not there when empty, and stands in front only where it could not be 260 wide beside 360.
9c. **One bar over a conversation**: the agent and, at the far right, the panel's key; 26 from the top, where macOS sets its own buttons under a unified bar. **Stop is where one writes**: in place of sending while nothing is written, beside it otherwise; a click meant for the one never does the other. It has no views and opens no terminal. Limits are an account's and stand over Accounts. Where it is stands in the sidebar, and in the bar only when that is put away. **What is not needed is not shown**: keys of the window when a row is under the hand, an agent's doing while it does something, a folder's plus when the hand is there. A level that stands beside a figure is a ring.
9a. **Every page has a way back**: Cancel (a cross) or Done, and Escape. What is shown is what is selected in the sidebar, and nothing else is.
9b. **The words other tools use**: Refresh, Discard, Apply, Update, Cancel, Remove, Comment. Not "look again", "throw away", "bring in".
10. **Motion** answers a press: springs (`snap`, `slide`, `glide`) that can be interrupted; nothing waits for them; reduced motion jumps. Only a running lamp moves by itself.

9e. **A new session is a conversation that has not begun**: the composer, in the middle of an empty page, with what it runs on and how much it may do in it, and where it works under it; Enter begins it. No form. It begins as its person last worked, except how much it may do. What is missing is said in one line under it. With no session at all the window opens on it. A folder dropped on the window, or asked for in a terminal, opens it there; while one is held over the window the canvas says where it would go, and nothing else on it. Another agent is added the same way, with a name over the words. Access is a key of its own wherever an agent is written to.
9d. **Things have the names people use.** A model is what its provider calls it, or else what its identifier plainly says (`model_name.dart`); the identifier is shown only to tell two apart, or if nothing can be made of it. A harness and an account by name, never by identifier.

## Roles
| Role | Use | Treatment |
|------|-----|-----------|
| `chassis` | the window, the sidebar | ground, flat |
| `canvas`, `bay` | what the window shows | one tone up, soft shade, round 12 |
| `track`, `block` | troughs; code, diffs, a command that is asked about | recessed |
| `plate` | a request, what was asked, a group of rows | raised |
| `written` | the composer | raised, soft shade, the faint lit edge of what else is raised, round 16 |
| `slab` | what is chosen: a row, the thumb of a trough | raised |
| `key` / `keyDown` / `primary` | keys, held ones, the main act | raised / recessed / ink |
| `ghost` | a key of little weight, a tag | a veil |
| `field` | a field | a tone, and a ring with the keyboard |
| `overlay` | menus, search | raised, floating |

Radii: check 5, control 8, code 10, card 12, canvas 12, composer 16. Controls are 30 high, fields 32, icon keys square, the act of the composer round. A held key gives 3 %; a ground comes in 110 ms. Icons are Microsoft's Fluent (16, one pixel of line, round corners), as path data in `icon_data.dart`. Type 11–22, a little tight, with Inter's display cut as it grows. Density compact/regular/roomy changes control height (26/30/34), never type size.
States for every control: rest, hover, held, engaged, focused (keyboard ring), disabled (a main act and a key keep a hairline outline with grey words; a quiet key is flat words), loading, error.
Keys of the window: new session N, search K, accounts comma, sidebar B, panel D, zoom + − 0, with ⌘ on macOS and Ctrl elsewhere. Text size has no keys of its own; it is found by the search.

**A request** is shown whole, spelled out, and cannot be allowed before it has been seen to its end.
