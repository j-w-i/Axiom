## Found and fixed
- **Updates were lost at a turn's end.** Responses resolve their waiter directly, while
  notifications are queued, so a prompt response could be seen before the updates the agent
  sent just before it. The select is now `biased` with the queue first (the reader queues
  before it resolves).
