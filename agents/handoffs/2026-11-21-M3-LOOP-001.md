## Decisions and gaps
- **Approvals are not journalled as `approval`/`approvalResolved` events.** The gate tells
  approving connections directly (`agent/approvalRequested`/`Resolved`). The decision's rule is
  in `toolResult.rule`, and the full record is in the audit log. `sessionOpen` returns the
  pending ones. The two event kinds stay in the protocol for a later task.
- **Lagging clients.** Broadcast capacity is 4096 events per session. A client that falls
  further behind loses events (logged); it can `sessionOpen` again.
- **ACP providers are refused by `sessionCreate`** (`unsupported`) until M3-ACP-001.
