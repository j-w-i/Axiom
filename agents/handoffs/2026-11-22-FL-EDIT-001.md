## Result
Task status: **implemented** (Linux; CI pending).

- **`re_editor` passed the frame gate first.** On a 5000-line Rust file at the owner's
  4080×2240 physical size, no frame went over 16.7 ms while scrolling, typing or moving the
  cursor (`verification/FL.md`). So it stays; the ADR's fallback, Axiom's own editor widget, is
  not needed.
- **`lib/state/documents.dart`**: the Tauri store's rules, without the bridge.
  - Saving sends `expectedVersion`; a conflict goes to a queue (Overwrite, Reload, Cancel).
  - External changes are decided by the version.
  - Unsaved-work prompts for closing a tab, switching or closing the folder, and quitting.
  - Tab order, cycling and moving.
  - The right group.
  - Persistence in `editor.*`, restored when the folder reopens.
  - Notifications that the controller triggers during a build are deferred to after the frame.
  - Opening by keyboard focuses the editor; a click in the explorer leaves focus there.
- **`lib/editor/code_view.dart`**: `re_editor` styled from the tokens.
  - Syntax colours are tokens, checked at 4.5:1 or better on the editor ground in both themes.
  - 17 languages through `re_highlight`.
  - Line numbers; the editor's own Mod+S routed to the store.
- **`lib/editor/editor_area.dart`**:
  - tabs with dirty and changed-on-disk marks, middle-click to close, and the close button;
  - breadcrumbs and the banner;
  - one editor per document, so tab switches never swap a model;
  - the right group with a splitter;
  - a find and replace bar built from Axiom's controls (case, regex, count, next, replace,
    replace all);
  - the cursor reported to the status bar, and reveal at line:column.
- **Shell**:
  - commands and keys: Mod+S, Mod+Alt+S, Mod+W, Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab,

## Deviations
- **The right group has its own controller.** `re_editor` binds one editor per controller;
  sharing one made the first editor rebuild during the second's build. The two controllers
  share the immutable text, so each view has its own caret and its own undo history. Monaco
  shared one model.
- **No tab dragging yet.** Moving a tab works by keyboard (Ctrl+Shift+PgUp/PgDn).
- **No crash recovery of unsaved text.** The Tauri shell's T-08 restored text after the
  webview's process died; the Flutter shell has no separate web process.
- **Text in the end-to-end suite** is typed through the document's controller: simulated key
  presses carry no text input on desktop. Commands use their real keys.
