# M1.6-INTERACTION-004: Reorder editor tabs by drag

## Objective
Editor tabs can be reordered by dragging with the pointer and by keyboard
(Ctrl+Shift+PageUp / PageDown), with lift, sibling shift and settle motion; the new
order persists across restart through the existing tab-state save.

**Gate: decision D7** (`docs/design/concepts.md` §6). If the owner rejects D7, this task
is set to `blocked` with `status_note: cancelled by D7 (owner, <date>)` and QA-006 drops it
from its dependencies.

## Context
`docs/design/motion-system.md` §4 rows "Tab reorder (D7)" and "Drag-and-drop";
`docs/design/workspace-spec.md` §2.3 (tab strip). The documents store already persists
open tabs through `saveTabState()` (`src/state/documents.ts`, debounced), so order needs
no new persistence.

## Required interfaces
- `DocumentsStore` (`src/state/documents.ts`): snapshot `docs` order, `activate`, `cycle`,
  `saveTabState` (existing).
- Editor bridge 1 (`docs/protocols/editor-bridge.md`) and `editor/src/documents.ts`: **untouched**.

## Implementation requirements
1. Add one store action `move(path: string, toIndex: number): void` that reorders the
   snapshot's `docs` array, emits, and schedules the existing debounced tab save. No
   other store change. `cycle()` follows the new order.
2. Pointer drag on a tab (not on its close button): starts after 4 px movement
   (`setPointerCapture`). The dragged tab gets the overlay shadow and `scale(1.02)`,
   follows the pointer on X only (`translateX`), clamped to the strip.
3. Siblings shift by the dragged tab's width with `transform: translateX(±w)` over `--dur-2`
   `--ease-out` when the pointer crosses their midpoint. A 2 px `--accent` insertion line
   marks the drop slot.
4. Drop: the dragged tab settles to its slot over `--dur-2`, then `move()` commits in one
   React update and all transforms reset in the same frame (no flash). Escape during drag
   cancels and settles back. Dropping outside the strip cancels (no tear-off).
5. Keyboard: Ctrl+Shift+PageUp/PageDown on the focused tab moves it by one; an
   `aria-live="polite"` region announces "executor.rs moved to position 2 of 4".
6. The glide (INTERACTION-003) hides during drag and reappears on the active tab after drop.
7. Strip auto-scrolls when dragging within 24 px of its edges (8 px per frame).
8. Reduced/Off: no lift scale or sibling travel; slots swap instantly; the insertion line stays.

## Constraints
- No change to `editor/src/`, the bridge, the protocol or `src-tauri/`.
- No drag-and-drop library; pointer events only.

## Non-goals
- Dragging tabs between split groups or out of the window (M4 split panes).
- Dragging files from the explorer.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Dragging a tab reorders it; the order survives an app restart.
2. Keyboard reordering works and is announced.
3. Escape cancels a drag with the order unchanged.
4. Middle-click close, close button, Ctrl+W and Ctrl+Tab behave as before.
5. All suites pass; `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Tests
- Vitest `tests/stores.test.ts`: `documents.move reorders and saves tab state`,
  `cycle follows reordered tabs`.
- e2e `parity.mjs` or `smoke.mjs`: `drag tab reorders and persists across restart`,
  `ctrl+shift+pagedown moves tab`, `escape cancels tab drag`.
- `pnpm test`, `pnpm run check`, `scripts/e2e.sh`, `scripts/check.sh`.

## Visual verification
S4 after a reorder (dark, light); a recording of drag, cancel and keyboard moves at
Standard and Off (`visual-qa.md` §6). §7 checklist.

## Performance
P3 during drag (rAF intervals while dragging 40 steps, p95 ≤ 17.5 ms, none > 25 ms); P5
unaffected. One React commit per drop.

## Failure conditions
- Any change outside the scoped reorder action in `documents.ts`.
- Lost or duplicated tabs after a cancelled drag.
