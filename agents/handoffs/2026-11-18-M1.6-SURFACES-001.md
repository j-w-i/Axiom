## Found: a localStorage write made less than about 1 s before quitting is lost (WebKitGTK)
- WebKit syncs localStorage to disk on a timer of about 1 s.
- In smoke, quitting right after running a command lost the MRU entry. It was in `localStorage` before the quit and `null` after the restart.
- Smoke now waits 1.5 s after 10b, with a comment.
- The layout key (`axiom.layout.v2`) has the same exposure.
- A host-side fix, flushing web storage on the quit path, is a follow-up outside this task's scope.
