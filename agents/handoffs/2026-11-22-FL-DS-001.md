## Result
Task status: **implemented** (Linux; CI pending). See `docs/design/flutter/README.md`.

- **Tokens** (`lib/ui/tokens.dart`):
  - both themes' colours (tone-step surfaces, ink, edges, states, brass accent, ok, warn,
    danger);
  - metrics, type (IBM Plex, tabular figures);
  - motion: Standard/Reduced/Off and three springs with 1.5–4 % overshoot.
- **Theme scope** (`theme.dart`): `AxiomTheme`, with `context.axiom` and `context.colors`.
- **Surfaces** (`surfaces.dart`):
  - `Well`, `Raised` (lit edge and contact shade, painted), `Sheet` (one soft shadow);
  - `Groove`, `Hairline`.
- **Interaction base** (`pressable.dart`):
  - `Pressable`: focus, hover, pressed, Enter/Space, semantics with a role;
  - `HintScope`: the hint line;
  - `FocusRing`, shown for keyboard focus only.
- **Controls** (`controls.dart`):
  - buttons: `AxButton` (standard, quiet, danger), `AxIconButton` (tooltip with keycaps),
    `InkCircleButton` (spring);
  - `Keycaps`, `AxBadge`, `StatusDot` (cut-out ring), `AxCheckbox`, `AxToggle`;
  - `AxListRow` (raised when selected, optional groove), `Spinner` (smooth; still with motion
    off), `AnswerLine`.
- **Overlays** (`overlays.dart`):
  - `AxTooltip` (intent delay; keyboard focus);
  - `AxMenu` (arrows, Enter, Escape returns focus, click outside closes; headings visual);
  - `showAxDialog` (scrim, focus trap, Escape, focus returns);
  - `ToastHost` (live region, no focus).
- **Also:**
  - `tabs.dart`: `AxTabs`, with the ink underline springing to its place and ← → moving between
    tabs;
  - `splitter.dart`: `AxSplitter`, with drag, arrows ±16, Home, and value semantics;
  - `text_input.dart`: `AxTextInput`, with the name merged into the field's own node and the
    bezel style for the composer.
- **Icons** (`icons.dart`, `icon_data.dart`): the 34 in-house icons ported as data, drawn with
  `path_parsing` (MIT).
- **Fonts.** IBM's unmodified TTFs, from the release archives already recorded (SHA-256
  re-checked); see `assets/fonts/SOURCE.md`.
- **Gallery** (`gallery.dart`): every control, shown by the app until FL-SHELL-001.
