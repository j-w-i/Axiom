## Behaviour implemented
- **`parse.rs`:** `Parser::new(format, roots)`, `line(stream, text)` and `finish()`;
  `workspace_path(file, roots)`; `strip_ansi`. No I/O. The roots are passed in; the daemon gives
  the workspace's canonical root, which is also the task's working directory.
- **`run.rs`:**
  - `tasks(registry, root)`.
  - `prepare(registry, provider, task, root, env, inherited PATH)` → `SpawnSpec`: argv only,
    `${workspace}` expanded, any other `${…}` refused; cwd is the root; the env overlay comes
    from `project::process_env`.
  - `drive(task_id, events, format, roots)` → `BuildEvent`s: line splitting per stream, with
    lines that span chunks joined and lines over 4 MiB cut; batches every 100 ms with only new
    diagnostics; the final `complete: true` batch before `Exited`; it keeps draining if the
    consumer goes away.
  - `Counts`.
- **`axiomd` `handlers/build.rs`:** resolves the environment when `.axiom/project.toml` exists
  (a failure is the error and nothing starts), starts the task through `App.executor`, forwards
  the events after the response, and writes a `BuildRun` to the store at the exit.
- **`axiom-store`:** migration 3 `build_history`, `build_history_add` (keeps the newest 20 per
  root) and `build_history` (newest first).

## Deviations and decisions
- **"A final complete set on exit"** is the last batch with `complete: true`, holding what was
  not sent yet. The union of the batches is the full set, as frozen in `ipc.md` by
  M2-ARCH-001.
- **The build history is not exposed through the protocol.** ADR-0014 names no method for it;
  a later task can add one.
- **`build/run` does not write `.axiom/project.lock`**; `env/resolve` does.
- **Outside the declared scope:**
  - `crates/axiomd` (handlers, router, tests);
  - `crates/store` (migration 3);
  - `docs/architecture/modules.md` (status).
