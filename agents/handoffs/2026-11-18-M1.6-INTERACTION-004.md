Editor tabs reorder by pointer drag and by keyboard (Mod+Shift+PageUp / PageDown on the focused tab, announced). The new order persists across a restart through the existing debounced tab-state save.

## Behaviour implemented
- **`DocumentsStore.move(path, toIndex)`:** the only store change. It clamps `toIndex`, reorders `docs`, emits and schedules the existing tab save. `cycle()` follows the new order. Restore was already sequential, so the saved order comes back as saved.
- **`EditorArea.tsx`:**
  - `useTabDrag` drives the drag:
    - It starts after 4 px, then captures the pointer on the strip.
    - The lifted tab gets `data-lifted` (overlay shadow) and `translateX` plus `scale(1.02)` in Standard only. It is clamped to the strip.
    - Siblings shift by the dragged tab's width (`siblingShift`) over `--dur-2`.
    - A 2 px `--accent` `.tabs__drop` line marks the slot.
    - The strip auto-scrolls 8 px per frame within 24 px of its edges.
    - **Drop:** the tab settles over `--dur-2`, then a single `move()` commits, and a layout effect clears every transform in the same frame (no flash).
    - **Escape, `pointercancel`, or a drop more than a strip's height away:** the tab settles back with the order unchanged.
    - The click that ends a drag is swallowed. The close button never starts a drag.
    - Everything during the drag is written to the DOM, so React commits once, on drop.
  - **`dropSlot(lefts, widths, from, dx)`** (pure, tested) uses the dragged tab's **leading edge** crossing a sibling's midpoint. A first version used the centre, and a wide tab clamped at the strip's start could not pass a narrow first tab; smoke 22a caught this.
  - **Keyboard:** Mod+Shift+PageUp/PageDown moves the focused tab by one. Focus stays on it, and a polite live region (`.tabs__announce`, outside the tablist) says "README.md moved to position 2 of 2".
- **`tabs.css`:**
  - drag cursor, sibling and settle transitions (none in Reduced and Off);
  - the lifted tab in the editor tone with the overlay shadow (static, not animated);
  - the glide hidden while dragging, reappearing on the active tab after the drop through an instant placement;
  - the drop line.
- **`shell/shortcuts.ts`:** Mod+PageUp/PageDown (cycle) now requires no Shift, so Mod+Shift+PageUp/PageDown reaches the focused tab. This is outside the declared scope but necessary: the capture-phase handler swallowed the chord before.
