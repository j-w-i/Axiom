# The wire

Types: `engine/crates/proto`. Why: decision 0003. One JSON object per line, UTF-8, at most 8 MiB.

1. Connect to the Unix socket named in `<data>/run/endpoint`, on every system. The name is the host's to choose; nobody computes it. On Unix the host drops peers with another uid.
2. First line, at most 4 KiB: `hello` with the token in `<data>/run/token` and the protocol number. Anything else, a wrong token or another protocol gets one error and the connection closes. Silence closes it after 5 s. Over 64 clients, `hello` is answered `busy`; over 32 connections without it, the next ones wait to be accepted.
3. `{"id":1,"method":"session.list","params":{}}` → `{"id":1,"result":…}` or `{"id":1,"error":{"code":…,"message":…}}`. An unreadable request is answered with `id` 0 if it has none. Errors do not repeat what was sent. While more answers and events wait than the host keeps for one client, it reads no more requests.
4. `session.follow {session, after}` is answered, then `{"session":…,"events":[…]}` messages follow: history after `after` at the client's pace, then live, in order, nothing twice. History has holes where ended items' deltas were folded. A second follow of a session on one connection replaces the first: its answer comes before any of its events, and nothing of the first comes after that answer.
5. `{"session":…,"resync":N}` means the client fell behind, or, with `N`=0 and only then, that its `after` is ahead of this history. Nothing more comes for that session until it follows again: from its last `seq`, or with 0 from 0 after discarding what it has.
6. `{"session":…,"fault":"words"}` means the host cannot read or write the session. Nothing more comes for it; a client shows the words and does not follow again by itself. Asking for it (`session.follow`, `replay.start`) is a new try, answered with error `internal` and words while it does not work. `host.status` lists `faults` as `[{session, words}]` and, if a check of the journal's file after an unclean end found damage, `damaged` in words.
7. `session.list {limit, offset}` (200 and 0 if absent, at most 1000) is an array, last changed first. `running` means that this host runs something in it.
8. `host.shutdown` is refused with `busy` while agents run, unless `force`. A host that stops starts nothing, ends its runs, writes how they ended, sends it, and closes. `SIGTERM`, `SIGINT` and `SIGHUP` do the same with force.
9. What was sent is in the journal. `item.ended.text` is authoritative and replaces the deltas before it. A prompt's and a tool's `item.started`, `session.created`, `session.titled`, `agent.added`, `run.started` and `request.*` survive power loss once sent or answered; the rest survives the host.
10. Readers ignore unknown fields and keep unknown event types in sequence; an unknown enum value reads as `unknown`.

Lengths, in bytes as encoded in JSON, kept by the host before it journals: text of an item 1 MiB, its arguments and its result 256 KiB each, an approval's subject and a run's error 64 KiB, scopes and answers 4 KiB, a workspace 4 KiB, titles and names 500. Text that is cut ends with `[N more bytes were not kept]` (while its item runs, with `[more is not kept]`); arguments and results over the limit, or nested deeper than 64, become `{"truncated":N}`. `session.create` refuses a title or workspace over its limit. A message of events holds about 256 KiB, or one longer event.
