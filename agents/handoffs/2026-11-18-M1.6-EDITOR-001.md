The editor tab strip is now:
- a recessed chrome strip (32 px), with the active tab as a raised key in the editor's tone and no seam below it;
- marked by one brass lit edge, placed over the active tab by transform;
- showing the explorer's glyph chips, a dirty dot that becomes the close glyph on hover, and close glyphs only on the active, hovered or focused tab.

A 24 px breadcrumb row with path segments only sits between the tabs and Monaco. Tab semantics, test ids and keyboard behaviour are unchanged.

## Behaviour implemented
- **`EditorArea.tsx`:** split into `TabStrip` and `Breadcrumbs`.
  - `glideTransform(offsetLeft, width)` is exported and tested.
  - A layout effect places the edge and writes the transform straight to the DOM (no state). It also observes the strip and the active tab with a ResizeObserver and scrolls the active tab into view (`inline: "nearest", block: "nearest"`).
  - A vertical wheel scrolls the strip sideways.
  - The `changedOnDisk` badge is an 11 px `--warn` label.
- **Close slot:** the dot and the X share the 18 px slot (CSS grid). Visibility rules are in `tabs.css`. The accessible name is unchanged (`Close <title> (unsaved)`).
- **`Breadcrumbs`:** `nav aria-label="Location"` > `ol`, 12 px chevrons in `--text-faint`, the last segment `--text` with `aria-current="location"`. Shown while a document is active, so Monaco's container keeps its size between tabs.
- **CSS:**
  - `styles/components/tabs.css` is new and uses tokens only.
  - The old tab rules and the top `::after` accent bar were removed from `app.css`.
  - The glyph chip rules moved from `tree.css` to the shared `glyph.css`.
  - The focused-region rule on the strip keeps the bottom seam (`shell.css`).
